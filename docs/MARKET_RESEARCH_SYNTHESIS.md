# Market Research Synthesis (Roro OS)

## What wins in assistant products now
1. **Narrow wedge + deep utility** beats broad generic assistants.
2. **Fast time-to-first-success** (<5 min) drives retention.
3. **Cross-tool execution** (not just chat answers) is the moat.
4. **Trust + reliability** matter more than flashy demos.
5. **Proactive loops** (scheduled briefs/follow-ups) increase daily usage.

## Competitive pattern summary
- Chat-only tools: easy adoption, weak retention.
- Automation-first tools: strong retention, steeper onboarding.
- Winning hybrid: conversational front-end + deterministic execution engine.

## Roro positioning (recommended)
"Roro OS is your execution assistant: one command, multi-tool completion, measurable outcomes."

## Initial monetization path (later)
- Phase 1: free beta, optimize activation/retention
- Phase 2: paid Pro by automation volume + premium integrations
- Phase 3: team plans with governance + analytics

## First ICP
- Founder/operators with heavy inbox/calendar/task load.

## Feature priorities from market demand
1. Daily brief + action queue
2. Inbox triage + draft actions
3. Calendar-aware scheduling and follow-up automation
4. Reliable job execution with audit trail
5. Performance dashboard (time saved, tasks completed)
